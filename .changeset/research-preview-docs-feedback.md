Research preview: a `submit-docs-feedback` tool sends feedback on the documentation that `query-docs` returned (helpful or not, what was missing or wrong, and a short comment) from the agent or the user. It is listed only with research preview on: `--research-preview` or `CONTEXT7_RESEARCH_PREVIEW=1` for stdio, and `?research_preview=1` on the MCP URL for the hosted server.
